Reference · Sources

Every figure, its citation.

This site is an argument, but it's built on the public record. Below are the sources behind each page, grouped to match. Where a number is a government or company claim, a projection, or contested, it's labelled that way on the page it appears. Sources change their URLs; if a link rots, search the document title. Spot an error? Tell us and we'll correct it.

A note on method

  • verified — confirmed by a primary/official source or several reputable ones.
  • claim / approx — a government or company figure, a rounded number, or one that varies by source.
  • contested — disputed, projection-based, or an interpretation rather than a settled fact.

Several load-bearing figures are projections to 2060 (e.g. Sydney's toll bill) or company investor-material figures (e.g. per-road toll-escalation floors) rather than raw contract deeds, which remain commercial-in-confidence. We flag these on the page and again here. Treat every number as a starting point for your own check.
